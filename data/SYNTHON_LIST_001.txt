- `d`¹ Synthons: **Methyl anion** (`:CH₃⁻`). *Synthetic equivalent: CH₃Li, CH₃MgX*.
- `d`² Synthons: **1-Hydroxyalkyl anion** (`CH₂=CH-O⁻`). *Equivalent: Enamines, enol ethers*.
- `d`³ Synthons: **Homocholate / β-Acyl anion** (`⁻CH₂-CH=O`). *Equivalent: Silyl enol ethers, enamines*.
- Acyl Anion Equivalent: **`R-C≡O⁻`** . *Equivalent: Cyanide ion (CN⁻) for `⁻C≡O`, thiazolium salts in benzoin condensation*.
- Carboxyl Synthon: **`⁻COOH`** . *Equivalent: Cyanide ion (CN⁻), Grignard reagents + CO₂* .
- Alkynyl Synthon: **`R-C≡C⁻`** . *Equivalent: Acetylide anions (e.g., sodium acetylide)* .
- Allyl Synthon: **`CH₂=CH-CH₂⁻`** . *Equivalent: Allyl Grignard or allyl lithium reagents*.
- Benzyl Synthon: **`Ph-CH₂⁻`** . *Equivalent: Benzyl Grignard or benzyl lithium reagents*.
- Hydride Synthon: **`H⁻`** . *Equivalent: NaBH₄, LiAlH₄*.
- Enolate Synthons: **`R-C⁻=O`** . *Equivalent: Lithium enolates, silyl enol ethers*.
- `a`¹ Synthons: **Methyl cation** (`CH₃⁺`). *Equivalent: CH₃I, (CH₃)₂SO₄*.
- `a`² Synthons: **1-Hydroxyalkyl cation / Iminium** (`CH₂=CH⁺`, `R-CH=O⁺H`). *Equivalent: aldehydes/ketones under acidic conditions, iminium salts*.
- `a`³ Synthons: **β-Acyl cation** (`⁺CH₂-CH=O`). *Equivalent: α,β-unsaturated carbonyls (Michael acceptors), Mannich bases*.
- Carbonyl Cation: **`R-C⁺=O`** . *Equivalent: Acid chlorides, carboxylic acids under dehydrating conditions*.
- Alkyl Halide Synthons: **`R⁺`** for primary, secondary, or tertiary alkyl groups. *Equivalent: R-Br, R-OTs, R-I* .
- Allyl Cation: **`CH₂=CH-CH₂⁺`** . *Equivalent: Allyl halides*.
- Benzyl Cation: **`Ph-CH₂⁺`** . *Equivalent: Benzyl bromide (PhCH₂Br)* .
- Acylium Ion: **`R-C≡O⁺`** . *Equivalent: Acid chlorides + Lewis acid*.
- Iminium Ion: **`R₂C=N⁺R₂`** . *Equivalent: Condensation products of amines and carbonyls (used in Pictet-Spengler reaction)* .
- Carboxylic Acid Dimer (Cyclic): The classic example you mentioned, a robust hydrogen-bonded ring motif .
- Carboxylic Acid Catemer (Chain): An alternative chain motif where each acid group donates a hydrogen bond to the carbonyl of a neighbor.
- Hydrated Acid Dimer: A cyclic dimer that incorporates one or two water molecules into the hydrogen-bonding ring .
- Heterosynthon with Amide: The acid--amide dimer, where an acid hydrogen bonds to the amide carbonyl and the amide N-H bonds to the acid carbonyl.
- Heterosynthon with Pyridine: The acid--pyridine synthon, a very reliable motif for forming co-crystals.
- Imidazole--Carboxylic Acid: A specific heterosynthon observed in some caffeine co-crystals .
- Amide Dimer: A robust cyclic motif similar to the carboxylic acid dimer, common in primary amides.
- Amide--Amide Chain: A catemeric motif, often found in molecules that cannot easily form the dimer.
- Amide--Water Cluster: Incorporation of water molecules into the hydrogen-bonding network of amides.
- Hydroxyl--Hydroxyl Chain: An O-H···O-H catemeric motif.
- Hydroxyl--Ether: An O-H···O hydrogen bond between an alcohol and an ether oxygen.
- Hydroxyl--Carbonyl: An O-H···O=C hydrogen bond.
- Phenol--Phenol Herringbone: A common motif for phenols involving both O-H···O and C-H···π interactions.
- Halogen Bond (X-bond) Dimer: A motif where two molecules are linked by two reciprocal halogen bonds, e.g., N···I--C and I···N.
- Halogen--Carbonyl: A directional interaction between an electrophilic halogen (like I or Br) and a carbonyl oxygen.
- S···O Chalcogen Bond: A non-classical synthon involving a sulfur and an oxygen atom, which can be surprisingly robust .
- Se···N Chalcogen Bond: A directional interaction seen in organoselenium compounds like ebselen .
- Face-to-Face π-π Stacking: Parallel stacking of aromatic rings.
- Edge-to-Face (T-shaped) C-H···π: A common motif in benzene and many aromatic crystals.
- Offset / Herringbone Packing: A common motif for aromatic hydrocarbons without strong hydrogen-bonding groups.
- Nitrile--Halogen: A common motif in halogen-bonded co-crystals.
- Nitro--Amino (Push-Pull): Often leads to intense charge-transfer interactions and color.
- Nitro--Iodo: A specific type of halogen bond involving a nitro group's oxygen.
